# USMA ARCHIVE

**A Digital Historical Archive of USM Alger**

USMA ARCHIVE is a digital archive and historical database dedicated to **Union Sportive de la Médina d'Alger (USM Alger)**, founded in 1937.

The project aims to preserve, organize, and document the club's history through structured data about its players, coaching staff, squads, seasons, and honors.

## Project Status

**Current Version: V0.1**

V0.1 establishes the foundation of the archive, focusing on player records, staff information, season history, and club honors.

## Features

- **Player Management** — Manage player profiles and related records.
- **Staff Management** — Document coaches and other staff members.
- **Club Management** — Maintain club records and related information.
- **Season History** — Organize player and staff participation across seasons.
- **Staff History** — Track staff assignments and their periods at the club.
- **Squad History** — Browse USMA squads by season.
- **Honors History** — Document the club's titles and achievements.
- **Administrative Dashboard** — Manage and maintain the archive's data.

## Technology Stack

- **Backend:** Python, Flask
- **Database:** MySQL
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, Jinja

## Development Roadmap

### V0.1 — Foundation
Establish the core database and management features for players, staff, seasons, and honors.

### V0.2 — Matches and Results
Introduce teams, stadiums, matches, results, competitions, and match-related records.

### Future Versions
Expand the historical archive with additional documentation, photographs, lineups, match details, and data verification tools.

## Project Goal

The long-term goal is to build a reliable and structured historical reference for USM Alger, preserving the club's sporting history in a searchable digital format.

## Disclaimer

This is an independent historical archive project and is not presented as the official website or archive of USM Alger.
